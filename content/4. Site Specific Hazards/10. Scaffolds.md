1. General 
    
Before starting work on a scaffold, inspect it for the following: 

1. Are guardrails, toeboards, and planking in place and secure? 
    

2. Are locking pins at each joint in place? 
    

3. Are all wheels on moveable scaffolds locked? 
    

4. Do not attempt to gain access to a scaffold by climbing on it (unless it is specifically designed for climbing) – always use a ladder. 
    

5. Scaffolds and their components must be capable of supporting four times the maximum intended load. 
    

6. Any scaffold, including accessories such as braces, brackets, trusses, screw legs, ladders, etc., damaged or weakened in any way, must be immediately repaired or replaced. 
    

7. Scaffold planks must extend over their end supports not less than 6 inches nor more than 12 inches, unless otherwise specifically required. 
    

8. Scaffold platforms must be at least 18 inches wide unless otherwise specifically required or exempted. 
    

9. Where persons are required to work or pass under the scaffold, scaffolds shall be provided with a screen between the toeboard and guardrail, extending along the entire opening. 
    

10. All scaffolds must be erected level and plumb, and on a solid footing. 
    

11. Do not change or remove scaffold members unless authorized. 
    

12. Do not allow workers to ride on a rolling scaffold when it is being moved.  Remove or secure all materials and tools on deck before moving. 
    

13. Do not alter any scaffold member by welding, burning, cutting, drilling, or bending. 
    

For other rules and regulations regarding scaffolding, please refer Scaffolds, Chapter 296-874 WAC.
