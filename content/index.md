SAFETY AND HEALTH POLICY FOR BICKNELL BUILDS 

At Bicknell Builds, safety is a core part of everything we do. Our goal is to maintain a safe, healthy, and respectful work environment where no employee is expected to work under hazardous or unsanitary conditions. 

We believe that employees should take pride in and enjoy their work. Protecting the health and well being of our team is essential to that goal, which is why preventing workplace injuries and illnesses remains a top priority in all of our operations. 

Bicknell Builds is committed to providing ongoing safety training and maintaining a strong accident prevention program. Safety is a shared responsibility that starts with leadership and extends to every member of our team. Each employee is responsible for looking out for their own safety as well as the safety of their coworkers. 

By working together and making safe practices part of our daily routine, we can create a safer workplace and contribute to the well being of everyone on our team.
