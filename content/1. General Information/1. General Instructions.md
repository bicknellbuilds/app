1. Overview 
    
	Across the construction industry, safety is of paramount importance to everyone. From keeping workers safe and as comfortable as possible to ensuring our company runs efficiently and earns a positive reputation, it is in everyone's best interest to understand and follow the policies of this plan in order to establish and maintain an injury-free workplace environment. 

2. Procedures 
    This document contains guidance for safety procedures to be followed and forms to be used.  Supervisors are expected to integrate these procedures into the appropriate work activity and employees are expected to apply them on the job. The included forms are to be used if they apply to the job concerned. 

3. Dissemination 
    A copy of this statement will be issued to all management personnel

4. Regulations 
    A copy of the following documents will be maintained on each job site in the jobsite binder: 

	1.  Chapter 155, Construction Safety Standards from the Division of Industrial Safety and Health, Washington State Department of Labor and Industries. 

	2.  [A copy of this Accident Prevention Plan.](link to pdf) 

	3.  The [WISHA Poster, form F416-081-909](https://lni.wa.gov/forms-publications/F416-081-909.pdf), which tells employees and employers their rights under the Washington Industrial Safety and Health Act. 

	1. [Your Rights as a Worker, form F700-074-000](https://lni.wa.gov/forms-publications/F700-074-000.pdf), which tells employees about minimum wage, mealtimes and other rights they are entitled to. 

	2. [Notice to Employees – If an Injury Occurs, F242-191-909](https://lni.wa.gov/forms-publications/f242-191-909.pdf). 

	3. A copy of the completed [Site Specific Safety Plan.](link to SSSP)
