Procedure for Injury or Illness on the Job 

A.  Owner or lead person immediately takes charge 

1. [[2. Situations Involving Blood|Supervise and administer first aid as you wish]](Good Samaritan Law applies). 
    
2. Arrange for transportation (ambulance, helicopter, company vehicle, etc.), depending on the seriousness of the injury. Protect the injured person from further injury. 
    
3. Notify Chris or Project Manager, if not already present. 
    
4. Do not move anything unless necessary, pending investigation of the incident. 
    
5. Accompany or take injured person(s) to doctor, hospital, home etc. (depending on the extent of injuries). 
    
6. Take injured person to family doctor, if available. 
    
7. Remain with the injured person until relieved by other authorized persons (manager, EMT, doctor, etc.). 
    
8. When the injured person’s immediate family is known, the owner or supervisor should properly notify family members, preferably in person, or have an appropriate person do so. 
    

B.  Documentation 

1. Minor injuries – requiring doctor or outpatient care: After the emergency actions following an injury, [[5. Accident Investigation|an investigation of the incident will be conducted by the immediate supervisor and any witness to determine the causes.]]
    

[The findings must be documented on our investigation form.](link to investigation form) 

2. Major injuries – fatality, hospitalization, non-hospitalized amputation or loss of an eye(s):  Top management must see that L&I is notified as soon as possible, but at least within 8 hours of the incident that caused the fatality or in-patient hospitalization. Also, any non-hospitalized amputation or loss of an eye(s) must be reported to L&I within 24 hours of the incident. 
    

Call L&I at 1-800-423-7233 or visit the nearest L&I office or call OSHA at 1-800-321-6742. Top management will then assist the Department in the investigation. [The findings must be documented on our incident investigation report form](link to investigation form) and recorded on the OSHA 300 log, if applicable [(this will be required when we have more than 10 employees.)](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3746.pdf) 

C.  Near Misses 

1. All near-miss incidents must be investigated. Worker must complete [Incident Report Form](link to incident report form) and submit to Site Supervisor. 
    

2. Document the incident using [Incident Investigation Form](link to investigation form). 
    

3. Review the findings at the monthly safety meetings or sooner if the situation warrants.
